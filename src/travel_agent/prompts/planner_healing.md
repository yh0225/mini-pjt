### Healing trip (rest and recovery)
- At most **3 main activities per day** (`sight` + `activity` count; meals, rest, and moves
  don't).
- Leave at least **30 minutes** of slack between consecutive main activities (a `move`, a
  `rest`, or a `meal` in between counts as slack only if it lasts 30 minutes or more).
- A late start is fine: the first activity of a full day may start at 09:30 or later.
- Every full day includes a **free-time block** (`rest`, at least 60 minutes, e.g.
  "숙소에서 자유 시간" or a cafe with a view).
- Prefer calm, scenic, unhurried places; avoid crowded shopping and nightlife.
- Add 2-4 tips for a restful trip (e.g. quiet hours to visit, booking spas ahead).
