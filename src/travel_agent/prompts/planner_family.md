### Family trip (with children)
- At most **3 `move` activities per day**. Keep each day in one area.
- If the youngest child is **6 or younger**: every full day has a nap/rest block of at least
  **60 minutes** between 12:30 and 16:00 (`rest` at "숙소" or a quiet candidate place).
- Every day ends by **20:00**.
- Lunch (`meal`) starts between **11:30 and 13:00** every full day.
- Prefer places suited to the children's ages; avoid long hikes, bars, and late nights.
- Add 2-4 practical tips for parents (e.g. strollers, kids' menus, sun protection).
